# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 26, 2023**

Kristián Chovančák ("we", "our", or "us") operates the **Media Controls Widget** mobile application (the "Service").

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
The Service is designed to provide a home screen widget to control media playback on your device. To function correctly, it accesses certain types of data:

### Media Metadata and Playback State

The Service uses the Notification Listener Service to detect active media sessions on your device. This allows the widget to:

• Display the title and artist of the currently playing media.

• Show album art associated with the media.

• Display and update playback progress (duration and current position).

• Provide playback controls (Play/Pause, Next, Previous).

**Important**: This data is processed locally on your device in real-time. It is used solely to populate the widget UI and is not stored permanently, uploaded to any servers, or shared with third parties.

### Package Information

The Service may query the list of installed applications (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES) to allow you to select a "Primary Player" or to ignore specific apps from appearing on the widget. This information remains on your device.

## 2. Permissions
To provide its core features, the Service requires the following permissions:

• Notification Access: Required to read media metadata and control playback via MediaController.

• Query All Packages: Required to identify and list media player applications installed on your device for user configuration.

You can revoke these permissions at any time through your device's Android Settings.

## 3. Data Storage
The Service uses local storage (Shared Preferences) to save your widget configuration, such as:

• Selected Primary Player.

• List of ignored players.

• UI preferences (Theme, Album Art rotation).
This data is stored locally on your device and is not synchronized with any cloud services.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The Service interacts with third-party media players (e.g., Spotify, YouTube, etc.) installed on your device to facilitate playback control. We do not control how these third-party applications handle your data. Please refer to the privacy policies of your respective media player applications.

## 5. Security

The Service operates exclusively with local data storage, the security of your information is protected by the security measures implemented on your mobile device's operating system. 

We do not transmit data over the internet; therefore, the risks associated with data interception during transmission are not applicable to this Service. We recommend that you maintain the latest security updates for your device to ensure the integrity of locally stored data.

## 6. Children’s Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
