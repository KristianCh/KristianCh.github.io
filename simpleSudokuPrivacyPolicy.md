# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 26, 2023**

Kristián Chovančák ("we", "our", or "us") operates the **Simple Sudoku** mobile application (the "Service").

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personal information or data that can identify you. 

### Local Data Storage
The Service uses local storage (such as `shared_preferences`) to save your game progress, settings, and high scores directly on your device. This data is not transmitted to us or any third parties.

## 2. Types of Data Collected

### Personal Data
While using our Service, we do not ask you to provide us with any personally identifiable information.

### Usage Data
We do not collect information on how the Service is accessed and used ("Usage Data"). No analytics or tracking services are integrated into the application.

## 3. Third-Party Services

The Service does not use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

## 4. Security of Data

The Service operates exclusively with local data storage, the security of your information is protected by the security measures implemented on your mobile device's operating system. 

We do not transmit data over the internet; therefore, the risks associated with data interception during transmission are not applicable to this Service. We recommend that you maintain the latest security updates for your device to ensure the integrity of locally stored data.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top.
